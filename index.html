Wisdom of the crowd

The average predicted position for each team across every submitted table, compared with where they actually finished.

Every predictor ranks all 20 teams 1st to 20th before the season starts. For each team, we take the difference between where it actually finished and where it was predicted — call that delta.

Accuracy for that team is (19 − delta) / 19. Predict a team's exact final position and you get full marks for it. Predict Liverpool to finish 20th when they actually win the league, and you get zero for it.

A predictor's overall score is the average of their per-team accuracy across all 20 teams, so every team counts equally.
